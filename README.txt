She&Me static landing page

1. Open index.html in a browser.
2. Upload index.html, style.css, script.js and assets/ to your hosting root.
3. The contact form is demo-only. Connect it to your backend/CRM/email endpoint in script.js.
4. Marketplace buttons currently use placeholders (#); replace them with your actual OZON/WB/store URLs.
5. Google Fonts are loaded from the internet; replace with local fonts if a fully offline package is required.
